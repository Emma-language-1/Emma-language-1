# Hi, I am Yuyan Yin

I am a first-year PhD student in Language Science and Technology at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My work sits at the intersection of language assessment, educational NLP, AI-assisted writing, and human-centred evaluation.

My master's research examined how AI tools influenced second-language collaborative writing. I collected and scored 284 essays from 58 students across one semester, analysed the results using mixed methods, and wrote the resulting manuscript. I am now extending this interest toward rubric-grounded writing evaluation, feedback generation, and responsible AI for language learning.

## Current focus

- Quantitative and mixed-methods research
- Language assessment and learner-facing AI
- Language evaluation tools for children with autism

## Selected project

### Rubric Writing Evaluation Lab

A reproducible demonstration of multi-trait writing assessment. The project includes:

- rubric-based scoring for task response, organisation, language use, and evidence;
- quadratic weighted kappa and agreement diagnostics;
- rubric retrieval for evidence-grounded feedback;
- class-level error clustering;
- preparation of SFT and preference-pair data; and
- a provider-neutral adapter for local OpenAI-compatible model endpoints.

The demonstration uses synthetic data and is designed to make evaluation assumptions and limitations explicit.
